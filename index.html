The Detection Bias

Transit photometry detects planets by watching a star dim as a planet crosses in front. Giant planets block lots of light and are easy to spot but earth sized planets barely dim the star at all, so transit photometry instruments miss them.

 

SIGNAL 100.0%
DETECTION WAITING
Hot Jupiter — 1.02% drop · DETECTED Earth-like — 0.0084% drop · MISSED

Transit depth  =  (Rp / Rs)2 × 100

Hot Jupiter (11 / 109)2 × 100 = 1.02% DETECTED
Earth-sized (1 / 109)2 × 100 = 0.0084% = 84 ppm MISSED

The demo demonstrates a block which is exaggerated so you can see it. In reality an Earth sized dip at around (84 ppm) sits below the 1000 ppm floor of ground telescopes and right at the edge of even the most advanced telescopes of Kepler and JWST telescopes.

What this proves

  • Transit photometry is size biased and it favors big gas giants.
  • Earth-like planets are nearly invisible to current instruments.
  • These are the exact reasons why exoplanet needs four advanced detection methods

The Four Effective Detection methods

Four Main detection methods

These four detection methods help eliminate the size bias that makes Earth-like planets invisible to the main current detection methods. Each one feeds a specific input into the ExoRank algorithm so as next generation telescopes deliver this crucial data, every ExoRank score becomes more reliable.

Asteroseismology → Stellar Type Microlensing → Distance Spectroscopy → Biosignature Direct Imaging → Radius & Tidal Lock
Variable 4 · Stellar Type

Asteroseismology

The study of vibrations inside stars. Scientists measure sound waves trapped in a star to determine its age, stability and radiation output. Revealing if a star is safe enough to support civilization without needing any planet signals at all.

Variable 3 · Distance

Gravitational Microlensing

When a massive star passes in front of a distant star it’s gravity bends and magnifies the light behind it. If there is a planet orbiting that star it creates an extra spike of brightness which revals Earth sized worlds thousands of light years away that current methods are not capable or reaching.

Variable 7 · Biosignature

Atmospheric Spectroscopy

As starlight passes through a planets atmosphere different gases like oxygen and methane absorb specific colors of light. When analyzing what colors are blocked it is possible to determine exactly what the air is made of and it’s the only current method capable of confirming whether a planet has breathable air or not.

Variables 2 & 5 · Radius & Tidal Lock

Direct Imaging

Coronagraphs physically block out the blinding light of a host star which allows the planet itself to be directly photographed. This gives us the planet's true physical size and reveals whether one side is permanently facing its star in a tidal lock.
